This is a massive update with many rendering-related additions (spotlight shadows, global lighting,
bloom, MSAA to name a few), a new physics Mover for controlling characters, improved 3D sound
spatialization, redesigned scripting component callbacks, a new Writing Gameplay manual section and
the usual batch of bug fixes.

Moreover, the 01-physics sample has been updated to showcase several new features. Play it on your
browser at [https://www.crownengine.org/play](https://www.crownengine.org/play).
